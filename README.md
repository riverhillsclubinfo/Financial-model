# River Hills Club Info

An independent, member-built cash-flow model of the proposed River Hills capital plan, and a side-by-side comparison with the Concert offer.

- **Interactive model:** `index.html`. Change any assumption and every figure updates.
- **Infographic:** `images/`

Figures come from the Board's September 2026 capital plan presentation, the Club's long-range capital plan, the certified May 2026 vote, and the published summary of the Concert proposal. Member counts are estimates. This is not a Club document; please verify figures against Club records.
